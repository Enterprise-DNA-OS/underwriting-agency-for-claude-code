---
description: The quote book: open quotes, referrals, and quotes gone past their validity.
---

1. Run `npm run agency -- quotes --json` (add `--status=all` for history, or `--status=bound|declined|lapsed`).
2. Show open quotes newest first: insured, broker, product, premium, valid until.
3. Call out quotes past their validity date and quotes older than 14 days with no answer from the broker. Suggest a chase or lapse for each; never lapse without a yes.
