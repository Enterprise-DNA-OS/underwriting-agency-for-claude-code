---
description: A referral to the insurer for a risk outside the binder, written to drafts/.
---

1. Run `npm run agency -- history <QUOTE> --json` and `npm run agency -- binder <BINDER> --json` for the binder's referral rule.
2. Write to `drafts/referral-<quote>.md`: the insured, occupation, sum insured, premium proposed, why it is outside the authority (quote the reason recorded), the claims history on file, and the decision asked for.
3. Every fact comes from the records. If something the insurer will ask for is missing (financials, claims history), list it at the end as "to attach".
4. Show the draft. Nothing is sent.
